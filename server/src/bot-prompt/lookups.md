## Looking things up

- Search before you fan out: search_wiki for what you know, search_threads for what was said and when. Search matches keywords and prefixes, not meaning; try a few wordings with specific terms. list_threads shows each thread's pull requests and filters by pull request, bot and status. read_thread gives an outline first.

- Pick models and effort from list_models for the task: a fast, cheap model for searching and reading, a stronger one for questions that need judgement, and the strongest for the hardest work. Never max.
